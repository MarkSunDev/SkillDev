# Four-page store legal set

Produce four English pages for a US-market store submission. The pages describe
the same build and must agree with the Data safety declaration and the app
config.

| Page | Primary job | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| Terms of Service | Contract, conduct, purchases, termination, disputes | General contact |
| Privacy Policy | Data categories, purposes, sharing, rights, retention | General contact (or privacy@) |
| Child Safety Policy | 18+, CSAM zero tolerance, reporting, authorities | Dedicated child-safety email |
| Account Deletion | In-app path + public web request path | General contact |

Keep the pages distinct in wording and visual treatment. Do not publish four
near-identical templates.

## Terms of Service

Cover, in this order or an equivalent structure:

1. **Acceptance and eligibility.** 18+; legal capacity; the date/version.
2. **Accounts.** Sign-in model (device, email, phone, username); accurate
   information; account security; one account per person; our right to suspend.
3. **License and restrictions.** Limited, revocable, non-transferable license;
   no scraping, reverse engineering, automation, or interference.
4. **User content.** Ownership; license to host/process/display; representations;
   prohibited content; moderation and removal.
5. **Prohibited conduct.** Harassment, impersonation, minors, non-consensual
   content, illegal content, spam, commercial solicitation, circumvention.
6. **Reporting, blocking, and moderation.** In-app report/block; review; content
   removal; warnings, restrictions, suspension, termination; appeal route.
7. **Purchases and subscriptions.** Free or paid; auto-renewal; billing through
   the app store; cancellation; refunds; virtual items/coins and their loss on
   termination. If the build is free with no IAP, say so and keep future-version
   wording conditional.
8. **Account termination and deletion.** Our termination rights; user deletion
   path; in-app path; web request path; cancellation/restore window; what is
   deleted and retained; subscription cancellation is separate; logout and
   uninstall are not deletion.
9. **Safety and interactions.** No guarantee that users are who they claim to be;
   no criminal background or identity verification unless actually performed;
   assume risk when meeting offline; report safety concerns.
10. **Intellectual property and DMCA.** Our IP; user license; copyright takedown
    procedure and designated contact/channel.
11. **Third-party content and app stores.** Ads, links, external services, and
    the platform store's own terms.
12. **Disclaimers.** "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE"; no warranty of uninterrupted or
    error-free service.
13. **Limitation of liability.** Cap; exclusion of indirect/consequential damages;
    carve-outs required by law.
14. **Indemnification.** User indemnifies the operator for misuse and content.
15. **Governing law.** Use the confirmed state; do not default to Delaware
    without asking.
16. **Dispute resolution.** Informal notice/conference; arbitration provider,
    venue, fees, opt-out window, class/jury waiver, mass-arbitration process if
    required. If arbitration is not used, say so and identify the forum.
17. **Changes to the Terms.** Notice method; effective date; continued use.
18. **Termination and survival.** Sections that survive.
19. **Electronic communications.** Consent to electronic records and notices.
20. **Miscellaneous.** Severability, entire agreement, assignment, no waiver,
    force majeure, headings.
21. **Contact.** General contact email or form.

## Privacy Policy

Cover:

1. **Controller and contact.** Developer legal name, app/store name, general or
   privacy contact, DPO if one exists, postal address if provided.
2. **Scope.** Which app, sites, and services the policy covers.
3. **Data we collect.** Account/profile, user content, photos, messages, device
   and network data, usage, approximate location, purchases, reports/support,
   sensitive data the user chooses to provide (e.g. sexual orientation,
   ethnicity, beliefs), and data from third parties/social sign-in.
4. **Sources.** You, your device, other users, service providers, social
   platforms, app stores.
5. **How we use data.** Service operation, matching/feed/chat, safety and
   moderation, payments, support, analytics, legal compliance, corporate
   transactions.
6. **Legal bases.** Include only if EU/UK users are in scope; otherwise omit or
   keep a short qualified paragraph.
7. **How we share.** Service providers and processors, other users, legal/safety
   recipients, corporate transactions, with your direction.
8. **Sale, sharing, targeted advertising.** State the qualified position that is
   true for the build; provide the opt-out mechanism (settings, "Your Privacy
   Choices", GPC, authorized agent) where required. Do not write an absolute
   "we do not sell" unless it is true and approved.
9. **Cookies, SDKs, identifiers.** Cookies/pixels/SDKs used by the app and
   providers; advertising identifiers; how to reset/limit them.
10. **Retention.** Category-level summary; state a period only if operations can
    meet it and legal approves. Distinguish account data, safety records,
    transaction/tax records, backups, and logs.
11. **Your rights.** Access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction,
    objection, opt-out, non-discrimination, appeal; CCPA/CPRA and other state
    rights that apply; identity verification; authorized agents.
12. **Account deletion.** In-app and web paths; cancellation/restore window;
    what is deleted/retained; link to the Account Deletion page.
13. **Children.** 18+ only; no knowing collection from children; COPPA; how to
    report an underage account.
14. **International transfers.** Countries/regions and safeguards, at category
    level.
15. **Security.** Administrative, technical, physical safeguards; no absolute
    security promise.
16. **Third-party links and services.** Their own policies apply.
17. **Changes.** Notice and effective date.
18. **Contact.** General/privacy email, DPO, postal address if provided.

## Child Safety Policy

Cover:

1. **Operator and app identity.** Developer legal name and the app name exactly
   as shown on the store.
2. **Position.** 18+ only; zero tolerance for CSAM, grooming, sexual exploitation
   of minors, and any content that sexualizes or endangers a minor.
3. **Age requirement and enforcement.** The actual age gate; what happens when
   an underage account is discovered; no claim of ID verification unless the
   build performs it.
4. **Prohibited content and conduct.** CSAM, sextortion, grooming, trafficking,
   solicitation, sexualized images of minors, and attempts to circumvent age
   checks.
5. **Reporting.** In-app Report on profiles/posts/chat; dedicated child-safety
   email; what to include; do not forward or redistribute suspected CSAM.
6. **Safety tools.** Report and Block; moderation; account restrictions.
7. **Authorities.** Preservation and disclosure where required; reporting
   apparent CSAM to NCMEC or the relevant authority; cooperation with law
   enforcement.
8. **Removal and termination.** Content removal, account bans, appeals.
9. **Updates.** How the policy changes.
10. **Contact.** Dedicated child-safety email first; general contact for
    non-CSAM questions.

The dedicated child-safety address must be a real, monitored mailbox. Do not use
the general feedback address, and do not invent an address that has not been
created.

## Account Deletion

Cover:

1. **Fastest route: in-app deletion.** Exact path (e.g. Settings → Delete account
   → Delete); what the user sees; confirmation.
2. **Web request route.** Public URL; no app install and no sign-in; for users
   who cannot access the app.
3. **Request method.** Google Form, hosted form, or email; the form fields;
   what happens after submission; reference number; expected follow-up.
4. **What is deleted.** Account/profile, photos, posts, matches/likes/visitors,
   messages where technically feasible, and other account-linked data.
5. **What may be retained.** Legal, safety, fraud-prevention, accounting/tax,
   dispute records, backups; do not use retained records to maintain an active
   profile.
6. **Timing and cancellation.** Cancellation/restore window; when deletion
   starts; backup cycle; what the user must do to cancel.
7. **Subscriptions and purchases.** Deleting the account does not cancel a
   subscription; cancel in Google Play/App Store; coins/virtual items are lost
   unless the terms say otherwise.
8. **Logout, uninstall, and pause are not deletion.** Explain each.
9. **Contact.** General contact email and the request form.
10. **Accessibility.** Mobile-first, readable, no login, no app required.

## Google Form deletion request

The standard integration is a **branded form on the Account Deletion page that
POSTs to the Google Form's `formResponse` endpoint**. Do not embed the Google
Form in an iframe and do not send users to a separate Google Form page.

- Create the form under a company-owned Google account, not a personal account.
- Fields: account email/contact email, account name/identifier, reason
  (optional), other details (optional). The page can add a hidden honeypot.
- Publish it; allow anyone with the link to respond; do not require Google
  sign-in or limit responses to the organization.
- Collect the `formResponse` endpoint and all four `entry.*` field IDs
  (`email`, `account`, `reason`, `details`).
- Keep our own form controls; on submit, POST to `formResponse` through a hidden
  iframe, then show a confirmation and reference number. Append the reference to
  the details value.
- Keep a direct email link as a fallback for browsers that block the POST, but
  do not make it the primary path.
- Disclose Google as a form/processing provider in the Privacy Policy.
- Assign an owner to monitor responses and process them within the stated
  timeframe. The form does not delete accounts automatically and does not send
  anything to Google Play.

See `google-form-deletion.md` for the operational steps.
